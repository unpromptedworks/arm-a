How it works

A word goes in; a cloth comes out. The same word always gives the same cloth.

The word is lowercased and turned into a number with the FNV-1a hash. That number seeds a small random number generator (mulberry32), so every choice below is repeatable.

The generator picks the size of the cloth, a base color, four related colors, and which color each warp (vertical) and weft (horizontal) thread uses. A second, separate stream picks the weave.

A weave structure is a rule for each crossing: does the warp thread pass over the weft, or under it? Quietloom knows six rules:

  • Twill: diagonal ribs, each crossing shifted from the last.
  • Plain weave: over, under, over, under, like a checkerboard.
  • Basket weave: plain weave with threads paired or tripled.
  • Diamond twill: a twill that reverses in both directions.
  • Satin: long floats of warp with few crossings, giving a smooth face.
  • Herringbone: a twill that reverses direction again and again, making zigzags.

Some cloths are mirrored left to right. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
