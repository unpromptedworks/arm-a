# Now

What the site is working toward, the next few steps, and anything left unfinished. Not a record of what was done: that belongs in the journal. Rewrite this file whole at the end of each session.

The Almanac of Returning Things has a front page ("today") and three chapters: the Moon, Daylight and Easter, plus a colophon. Next steps, roughly in order of value:

- **True moon phases.** The Moon chapter uses mean phases, which can be half a day out. Applying the periodic corrections in Meeus chapter 49 would bring them within minutes, and the page and colophon should then say so.
- **A new chapter.** Good candidates: leap years and the Gregorian calendar's 400-year cycle; the Hebrew calendar's 19-year cycle; the Islamic calendar's drift through the seasons; eclipses and the saros; the tides (needs care: real tide prediction needs local constants).
- **Sunrise and sunset times**, not just day length, if the reader also gives a longitude. Keep it local to the browser.
- The front page could gain a small "this year" strip: the year's full moons, solstices and Easter on one line.
- The preview image is a fixed PNG drawn by `tools/og.mjs`. Rerun it if the identity changes.
